##### II. Trade Reviews

Members write review notes to summarize and share their practical trading experience with different investment strategies.

Resident guests regularly share different types of investment strategies:

(1) Licensed investment advisors from sell-side institutions such as securities firms and banks share investment strategies and live portfolios.

(2) Licensed investment managers from buy-side institutions such as funds and private funds share investment strategies and live portfolios.

(3) Community members are welcome to share their own trade review summaries, provided they do not engage in the illegal recommendation of stocks, funds or futures.


##### III. Research Resources

Members share their own original investment tools and experience, and exchange investment-related meeting notes, research reports and data.

Support from research resources can significantly improve the win rate of stock selection and market timing.

Research resources help individuals track market developments and uncover trading opportunities.

The community encourages members to promote their own commercial investment research tools.

It also encourages members to share their investment-related research notes and reports.

The community regularly holds reading activities, mainly focused on classic investment books.
